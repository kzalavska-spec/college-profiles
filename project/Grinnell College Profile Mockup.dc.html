Overview

Grinnell College is a private institution that was founded in 1846. In the 2027 edition of Best Colleges, Grinnell College is ranked No. 16 in National Liberal Arts Colleges. It's also ranked No. 6 in Best Undergraduate Teaching.

It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 1,668 (fall 2025), its setting is rural, and the campus size is 120 acres. The student-faculty ratio at Grinnell College is 8:1, and it utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.

Grinnell College accepts the Common Application and has a test-optional admissions policy. The school's tuition and fees are $74,154.
